# Walid AI

[Walid AI](https://walid-ai.com/) is an independent multipurpose digital platform from Bangladesh for learning, productivity and entertainment.

## Games

- [Riddlex — The Guessing Detective](https://walid-ai.com/games/riddlex/): a browser guessing game for people, characters, animals and objects.
- [Snakes & Ladders — The Serpent King's Realm](https://walid-ai.com/games/snakes-and-ladders/): animated board game with a bot or 2–4 local players.
- [Ludu Mahir](https://walid-ai.com/games/ludo/), [Chess](https://walid-ai.com/games/chess/), [Tic Tac Toe](https://walid-ai.com/games/tic-tac-toe/) and [2048](https://walid-ai.com/games/2048/).

## Productivity tools

- [PDF tools](https://walid-ai.com/pdf/): 11 tools for merging, splitting, compression, editing, organization, page numbering, watermarking, signing, protection, OCR and PDF-to-Word conversion.
- [Image tools](https://walid-ai.com/image/): six tools for resizing, compression, format conversion, background removal and selected signature and watermark editing tasks.

## Learning and knowledge

- [Al-Quran & Islam](https://walid-ai.com/islam/): all 114 surahs with Arabic text and Bengali translation, introductions and source acknowledgments.
- [Education](https://walid-ai.com/sections/education/): study and learning resources.
- [History and literature](https://walid-ai.com/others/): Bengali historical narratives and literary works.
- [Sports](https://walid-ai.com/sports/): football and cricket information.
- [AI Chat](https://walid-ai.com/chat/): an additional conversational assistant.
