About Walid AI

Walid AI is a free website for everyday questions and common file tasks. The chat page can be used in English or Bangla. The interface calls the product Walid AI 1.0.

You can ask questions in chat, work with PDFs, edit or convert images, pack or unpack ZIP files, and play browser games. Chat and the PDF tools send the request to the backend at walid-ai-backend.onrender.com. Resize, compress, convert, signature-background removal, watermark removal, and ZIP tools run in your browser. Background removal also runs in the browser after it downloads its model. The games run in the browser and do not need an account.

The PDF tools are Merge PDF, Split PDF, Compress PDF, and PDF to Word. The image tools are Resize Image, Compress Image, Convert Image, Remove Background, Remove Signature Background, and Remove Watermark. The games are Riddlex, Ludu Mahir, Chess, Football Penalty, Tic Tac Toe, Snake, and 2048.

Walid AI is developed by Whakil Ahamed Shakil, an individual developer in Bangladesh. The site does not list a registered company, a street address, or a staff. Support email is support@walid-ai.com.

Our aim

The aim is a straightforward place to ask a question and finish a common file task without installing an app. Answers from the chat can be wrong. Tool results depend on the file and the browser. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before you rely on the site or send a sensitive file.
