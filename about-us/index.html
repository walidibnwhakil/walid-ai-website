About Walid AI

One Platform. More Possibilities.

Walid AI is a digital platform designed to make everyday tasks, learning, and information more accessible. Bringing together intelligent assistance, practical tools, educational resources, and interactive experiences, Walid AI offers a convenient space for users to explore, create, learn, and accomplish more.

What We Offer

AI Assistance — Explore ideas, find answers, and receive writing and learning support in English and Bangla.

Digital Tools — Work with PDF documents, edit and optimize images, and simplify everyday file-management tasks.

Education & Knowledge — Access learning materials, practice questions, and resources designed to support independent study.

Al-Quran & Islam — Read the Holy Quran with Bengali translations, explore Islamic teachings, and discover informative resources with source acknowledgements.

Sports & Entertainment — Follow football and cricket updates, enjoy interactive games, and explore a growing collection of stories, literature, and world history.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create an accessible, reliable, and user-friendly digital experience that brings useful services and meaningful knowledge together in one place.

We are committed to thoughtful design, continuous improvement, and making technology more approachable for everyday users.

Our Vision

To develop Walid AI into a comprehensive digital destination where technology, education, creativity, and knowledge come together to support people in their daily lives.

Founder & Developer

Walid AI was founded by Whakil Ahamed Shakil, a graduate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (B.Sc. in EEE) from Bangladesh.

With an engineering background and a commitment to innovation, he founded Walid AI to make digital tools, learning resources, and intelligent assistance more accessible through a unified platform.

Under his direction, Walid AI continues to evolve with a focus on quality, usability, and meaningful digital experiences.
