About Walid AI

One Platform. More Possibilities.

Walid AI is an independent digital platform bringing together intelligent assistance, practical online tools, educational resources, and interactive experiences in one accessible space.

Designed with simplicity and usability in mind, Walid AI aims to make technology, information, and everyday digital services easier to access.

What We Offer

  • AI Assistance — Intelligent support for questions, writing, learning, and everyday tasks.
  • Digital Tools — Practical solutions for working with PDF documents and images.
  • Education — Learning resources, study materials, and interactive assessments.
  • Al-Quran & Islam — Quranic resources, Islamic knowledge, and informative content with source acknowledgments.
  • Sports & Games — Football and cricket information alongside interactive browser games.
  • History & Literature — A collection of historical narratives, literary works, and traditional stories.

Our Mission

To make useful digital services and reliable knowledge more accessible through a simple, convenient, and continuously improving platform.

Our Vision

To build a trusted digital destination where technology, education, and creativity come together to support everyday learning and productivity.

Founder & Developer

Walid AI was founded and developed by Whakil Ahamed Shakil, a graduate in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (B.Sc. in EEE) from Bangladesh.

Combining an engineering background with an interest in technology and digital innovation, he established Walid AI with the goal of bringing practical services and educational opportunities together on a single platform.
