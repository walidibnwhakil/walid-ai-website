This link opens your default email application. Review the message and send it from that application. The contact page does not store the message on Walid AI’s server.

The Help button on the chat page does the same thing. It fills a draft to support@walid-ai.com with the category, subject, and message you typed, then asks you to press Send in your email app.

What to include

  • The page you were on, such as chat, a tool, or a game.
  • What you tried and what you saw, including any error text.
  • Whether you were signed in. For a chat-deletion request, name the Google account you used.

Do not send your password, a Firebase token, or files you do not need the operator to see. The site does not promise a reply time.
