Ludu MAHIR

Roll a six to leave the yard. Capture rivals on open squares, use safe squares, and bring all four tokens home.

Choose your color

Before rolling, tap your player name to choose a color.

How to play

Select 2, 3 or 4 seats, then choose bots, same-device friends or a mix. Roll a six to launch a token. Move by the exact die count; landing on an opponent on a regular square sends them to their yard. Star-marked safe squares protect tokens. A six or a capture grants another roll. Move all four tokens around the track and up your colored home lane; the first to finish all four wins.
