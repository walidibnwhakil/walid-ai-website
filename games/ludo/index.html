Ludo

Roll a six to leave the yard. Capture rivals on open squares, use safe squares, and bring all four tokens home.

✦ ✧ ✦

Red wins!

All four tokens made it home.

How to play

Select 2, 3 or 4 seats, then choose bots, same-device friends or a mix. Tap the active player's dotted die on the board to roll. If multiple tokens can move, tap one glowing token; a sole legal token moves automatically. A six launches a token from the yard and earns another roll. Move by the exact die count; landing on an opponent on an open square captures them, while star-marked safe squares protect tokens. Captures earn another roll. Move all four tokens around the track and up your colored home lane; the first to finish all four wins.
