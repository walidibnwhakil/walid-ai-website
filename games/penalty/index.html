Football Penalty

Choose one of three lanes to shoot or dive. In two-player mode, look away while the shooter chooses. Play five rounds, then sudden death if tied.

How to play

Choose Play the bot or Two players, then New game if you switch. Each turn is a shot at the left, center, or right of the goal. A goal counts when the shot and the dive are different lanes.

Against the bot, you shoot, then you dive to face the bot’s shot. In two-player mode the other person should look away while the shooter picks a lane, because the choice stays on this screen. Play continues until both sides have taken at least five shots and the scores are different. If the score is level after five shots each, play goes on one pair of shots at a time.

Nothing is saved. Refreshing the page starts over. The game does not need an account.
