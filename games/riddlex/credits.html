Riddlex photo credits

Riddlex plays in the browser. Guess pictures are not stored in this repository. When a guess has a free picture, the game loads it from Wikimedia Commons and shows the Commons licence and artist under the photograph. If there is no free picture, the detective writes the name and does not print a photo. Names that are only known through adult entertainment never receive a picture.

Commons pictures

Files are requested from Special:FilePath after the build checks Commons licence metadata. A picture is kept only when that metadata names a free licence such as CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, GFDL or public domain. The line under a printed photograph is that licence and the artist text Commons returned. Wikidata’s own data licence is described at Wikidata:Licensing.

Scene artwork

The menu, the detective, the cat and the office are the existing Riddlex illustrations. A light wisp from the pipe, the notebook, the handwriting and the printer motion are drawn in the browser on top of those pictures.
