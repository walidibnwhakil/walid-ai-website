Riddlex

Think of a character, animal, object or someone you know.

No personal information is collected.

How to play

Press Play, then choose Characters, Animals, or Objects. Think of one answer and keep it. Riddlex asks yes-or-no questions. Answer Yes, No, Probably, Probably Not, or Don’t Know. The keys 1 through 5 match those buttons in that order. When a guess appears, choose Correct or Wrong. Wrong rules that guess out and the questions continue. Change last answer takes back the previous reply. New Game starts over.

Characters can include a real person you know. The detective may ask whether you mean someone by their relationship to you, such as a relative or friend. Animals and objects use the matching question set. If the casebook cannot load, the page says so; choose the category again. Loading needs this website’s question files. Your answers are not sent to the chat backend, and this game does not save a score.

What the detective can and cannot do

  • The questions on screen are English. Bangla wording exists in the game’s data files and is not what this screen asks.
  • A guess is only as good as the question set. An obscure answer can end with “I don’t have enough evidence.” Retry starts a new case from that point’s Retry button when it is shown, or use New Game.
  • Photo credits, including the Library of Congress Einstein photograph, are on the credits page.

Short FAQ

Do I need an account? No. The privacy line on the game says no personal information is collected. The script does not write your answers to storage.

Can I play offline? The first load of a category downloads question files from this site. After that, it depends on the browser cache. This page does not include an offline install step.
