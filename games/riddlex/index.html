How to Play

  1. Choose Character, Animal or Object and think of one without revealing it.
  2. Answer the detective's questions using Yes, No, Probably, Probably not or Don't know.
  3. The detective will make a guess based on your answers.
  4. If the guess is wrong, select No and continue the investigation.
  5. If the guess is correct, confirm it to finish the round.

  3. আপনার উত্তরের ভিত্তিতে গোয়েন্দা অনুমান করবে।
