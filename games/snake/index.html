How to play

Press Start, then steer with the arrow keys, WASD, a swipe on the board, or the direction pad under Show direction pad. The snake moves on a 16 by 16 board. Eating the fruit grows the snake and raises the score. Turning back on yourself, hitting a wall, or hitting the tail ends the run.

The best score is stored in this browser under a local value named walid-snake-best. Clearing site data resets it. There is no account and no online scoreboard.
