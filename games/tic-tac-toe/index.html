Tic Tac Toe

Take turns placing X and O. Line up three marks across, down, or diagonally to win.

How to play

Choose Play the bot for a local one-player game, or Two players to take turns on this device. Each player places one mark in an empty square. The first to make a row of three wins; a full board with no row is a draw.
