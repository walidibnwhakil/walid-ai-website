# Others catalog pictograms: evidence and scope

The catalog uses visual approximations, not archival facsimiles. Do not call a subject illustration an original coat of arms. Most literary characters and some historical figures lack a documented unique personal emblem.

Attested period-specific examples:
- Rome: legionary aquila: https://romanarmymuseum.com/about/aquila-the-eagle/
- Byzantium: late Palaiologan cross (not universal Byzantine official flag): https://www.academia.edu/116638059/Late_Byzantine_Insignia
- Venice: St Mark winged lion: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/antiquity/article/chinese-identity-of-st-marks-bronze-lion-and-its-place-in-the-history-of-medieval-venice/70176EBEF6C480FA4246DC2F189D1F9B
- Seljuk eight-pointed star in architecture: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Seljuk_architecture
- Jerusalem Cross: associated with the Kingdom of Jerusalem, not every crusader army.
- Mongol: horsehair tuq/sulde standard: https://acta.bibl.u-szeged.hu/67901/1/altaica_053.pdf
- Mamluk: cupbearer blazon, not a state flag: https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/khamseen/topics/2024/the-history-of-the-blazon-in-the-mamluk-period/
- Ottoman: 19th-century crescent-and-star national flag, not the entire dynasty's original emblem: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flags_of_the_Ottoman_Empire
- Timurid: triangular three-annulett tamga on coins and seals: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Three_annulets_symbol_of_the_Timurid_Empire.png
- Mughal: lion-and-sun imperial standard in later period: https://iris.polito.it/retrieve/handle/11583/2901472/e384c433-4a46-d4b2-e053-9f05fe0a1d67/SSRN-id2788568%20-%20Mughal.pdf
- British: Union flag representing 1801 onward, not entire colonial history.
- Napoleonic French Empire: imperial eagle by 1804 decree: https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/objects/imperial-eagles-first-second-empires/
- Rashidun: no surviving standardized official flag for reliable reproduction: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rashidun_Caliphate_Flag.png
- Alexander: ancient coinage-themed illustration; no invented personal coat of arms: https://www.mdpi.com/2313-5778/5/2/43

The remainder are subject illustrations (cave, fort, banners, raft, bow, wheel, dhow, manuscript, lamp). They do NOT claim to be authentic historical insignia. Accessible SVG titles label each illustration accurately. Reassess source-specific design details before substituting any image presented as a facsimile.
