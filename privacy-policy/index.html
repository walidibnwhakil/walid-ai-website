Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

At Walid AI, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you access walid-ai.com.

Walid AI is independently developed by Whakil Ahamed Shakil, Bangladesh. A Bengali version is also available.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Walid AI, the following information may be processed:

  • Account Information: When you sign in, account identifiers and information necessary for authentication and account-related features may be processed.
  • Chat Conversations: Messages submitted through AI Chat are processed to generate responses. Signed-in users may have conversations saved to their chat history.
  • Uploaded Files: Documents and images are processed when you use the relevant tools. Some operations are performed directly in your browser, while others require online processing.
  • Usage Information: We measure website activity, including page visits, feature usage, active time, and approximate country or regional information. A browser identifier may be used to estimate unique visitors. These analytics do not include the contents of your messages or uploaded files.
  • Contact Information: When you contact us, we receive the information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed to deliver requested services, manage account features, improve website performance, provide support, prevent misuse, and maintain security. We seek to limit information processing to what is relevant to these purposes.

3. AI Services and Third-Party Providers

Certain features depend on external services for authentication, hosting, AI processing, and other technical operations. Information necessary to complete a request may be transmitted to the relevant provider. Depending on the provider's terms and practices, submitted information may be subject to automated processing, human review, or service improvement activities.

We encourage users not to submit passwords, financial credentials, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

4. Cookies and Browser Storage

Walid AI uses browser storage and, where required, cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, maintain preferences, and enable account features. Optional usage analytics help us understand how visitors interact with the website. These may include a browser identifier and approximate geographic information obtained through an external network-location service.

You can disable optional browser-based usage tracking through the control below. This does not necessarily disable essential authentication, security processing, or third-party advertising technologies.

5. Advertising

Advertisements may appear in selected areas of Walid AI. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their applicable policies and legal requirements. The AI Chat experience is intended to remain free of displayed advertisements.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps, and manage advertising preferences through Google Ad Settings.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Guest conversations are generally stored within the browser session. Signed-in conversations may be stored using account-related services and local browser storage. Daily aggregated usage statistics are generally retained for up to 90 days, while overall statistical totals may be retained longer in aggregated form.

Other information, including account records, support correspondence, and operational logs, may be retained for different periods depending on the purpose, operational requirements, and applicable third-party policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and operational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, and loss. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may manage browser-stored information through your browser settings, delete individual saved conversations where the feature is available, and contact us regarding your personal information.

You may also disable optional browser usage analytics on this device. This control does not disable services required for security or sign-in.

For data-related requests, contact support@walid-ai.com. Verification may be required before certain requests can be fulfilled.

9. Children's Privacy

Walid AI provides general-purpose and educational content. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of online services. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, practices, or applicable requirements. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
