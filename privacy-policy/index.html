Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Walid AI respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is handled when you visit walid-ai.com, how it is used, and the choices available to you. Walid AI is operated by Whakil Ahamed Shakil in Bangladesh. A Bengali version is also available.

Information we process

  • Chat and account information. We process the messages you submit to provide responses. If you sign in, account identifiers and saved conversations may be stored to make your chat history available.
  • Documents and images. Files you choose to submit are processed to perform the requested action. Certain features process files in your browser; others may securely transmit files to service providers for processing. Not every uploaded file is saved to your account.
  • Preferences and browser storage. We use browser storage to remember settings, guest conversations, and selected preferences. Essential sign-in services may also use cookies or similar technologies.
  • Usage information. We may measure page visits, feature use, and approximate regional activity to understand website performance. This includes country or regional estimates, not a record of your precise location in our usage reports.
  • Messages to support. If you contact us by email, we receive the details you choose to include and use them to respond to your enquiry.

How information is used

Information is used to deliver requested services, maintain account features, support users, protect the website, and understand how its features are used. We do not ask you to disclose information that is unnecessary for your request. Please avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive personal details.

Service providers and content processing

Selected features rely on third-party providers for sign-in, hosting, content processing, and related operations. Information relevant to your request may be shared with these providers as necessary to deliver the service. Their handling of information is also governed by their applicable privacy terms. Depending on those terms, submitted content may be subject to human review or used to improve their services.

Tools and games

Many image, document, and archive features process your files directly in your browser. Other operations require online processing. Browser games generally operate on your device and may save settings or scores locally. The specific features and processing methods may change over time.

Cookies and advertising

Walid AI uses browser storage for essential website functions and may display advertisements in selected sections. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display and measure ads, subject to their policies and applicable law. The chat experience is not intended to display advertisements.

For information about Google's advertising data practices, see How Google uses information from sites or apps. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ad Settings and browser privacy controls.

Data retention and security

Guest conversations are generally held for the browser session. Signed-in conversations may remain available until you delete them or request assistance with their removal. Browser preferences remain until you clear them. Daily aggregated usage information is generally retained for up to 90 days; overall totals may be retained in aggregate. Other records, including service logs and email correspondence, may be retained for different periods according to operational needs and third-party policies.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can remove browser-stored information through your browser settings, delete individual saved conversations from your chat history, and request help with personal information at support@walid-ai.com. We may need to verify your account before acting on a request. Data held independently by third parties is subject to their own policies.

Optional browser usage measurement can be disabled on this device.

Children's privacy

Walid AI serves a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under that age. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the website changes. The revised policy will be published here with a new effective date. For privacy enquiries or data-related requests, contact support@walid-ai.com.
