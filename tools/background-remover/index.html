Select a color in the background to make that color transparent. The image is processed in your browser and is not sent to the image service.

How to remove a background

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Click the background color in the image preview.
  3. Adjust the color tolerance, then continue and download a PNG.

Best results

This color-based tool works best with an even, plain background. Shadows, gradients, and background colors similar to the subject can leave edges or remove parts of the subject. It is not an AI subject-segmentation service.
