How to remove a small mark

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image.
  2. Drag over a small watermark or mark in the preview.
  3. Continue to fill the selection, then download the edited PNG.

Editing limits

The fill is an approximation, not a reconstruction of the hidden original. It works best for small marks on plain or softly changing backgrounds. Detailed textures, text, or large marks may remain visible. Use this tool only on images you own or have permission to edit.
